OCS markup rates (official OCS markup-rates table): Dried Flower +23% · Pre-rolls +29% · Infused Pre-rolls +29% · Oils +25% · Capsules +25% · Vapes +25% · Concentrates +25% · Edibles +25% · Beverages +25% · Topicals +25% · Seeds +25% · Accessories +20%. OCS.ca eCommerce: +39% above wholesale → HST (13%) → rounded up to nearest $0.05. Source: OCS Pricing Guide, Oct 2025.
Excise duty: CRA EDM6-3 (Mar 2025) — dried/fresh cannabis = higher of $1.00/g flowering material or 10% ad valorem; cannabis extracts / edible cannabis / cannabis topicals = $0.01/mg THC flat-rate, no ad valorem.
Annual regulatory fee: Health Canada (Apr 2025) — Standard licence: 2.3% of net cannabis revenue (min $23,000/yr); Micro licence: 1% on revenue ≤$1M, 2.3% above (min $2,500/yr). Applied as % of per-unit net revenue in COGS.
Provincial distribution models & markups: StratCann provincial overview · BCLDB Wholesale Pricing Guide (Jan 2026) · AGLC Markup Bulletin.
Health Canada product classifications: Schedule 4, Cannabis Act & Cannabis Regulations. Beverages = edible cannabis. Infused pre-rolls & sublingual oils = cannabis extracts. Capsules = cannabis extracts.
MSRP shown inclusive of HST / GST / PST for all provinces. Private model provinces (AB, SK, MB) show a floor estimate — final retail price is set by the retailer.
