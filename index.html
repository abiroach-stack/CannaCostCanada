OCS markup rates (official OCS markup-rates table): Dried Flower +23% · Pre-rolls +29% · Infused Pre-rolls +29% · Oils +25% · Capsules +25% · Vapes +25% · Concentrates +25% · Edibles +25% · Beverages +25% · Topicals +25% · Seeds +25% · Accessories +20%. OCS eCommerce: +39% above wholesale → HST (13%) → rounded up to nearest $0.05. Source: OCS Pricing Guide, Oct 2025.
Excise duty: CRA EDM6-3 (Mar 2025). Dried/fresh cannabis = higher of $1.00/g of flowering material or 10% of your selling price to the board. Cannabis extracts / edible cannabis / cannabis topicals = $0.01/mg total THC — no ad valorem component.
Annual regulatory fee: Health Canada (Apr 2025). Standard licence: 2.3% of net cannabis revenue (min $23,000/yr). Micro licence: 1.0% on revenue ≤$1M, 2.3% above (min $2,500/yr). Applied as % of per-unit net revenue.
Provincial distribution models: StratCann overview · BCLDB Wholesale Pricing Guide (Jan 2026) · AGLC Markup Bulletin. All MSRPs inclusive of HST/GST/PST. Private model provinces (AB, SK, MB) show floor estimate only.
Health Canada product classifications: Schedule 4, Cannabis Act & Cannabis Regulations. Beverages = edible cannabis. Infused pre-rolls & sublingual oils = cannabis extracts. Capsules = cannabis extracts.
For estimation purposes only. Verify all figures with your accountant and regulatory counsel before submitting to any provincial board.
