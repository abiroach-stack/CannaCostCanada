# CannaCost Canada

**CannaCost Canada** is a Canadian cannabis pricing calculator built for licensed producers, brand operators, category managers, consultants, and retail-facing commercial teams.

It helps users model cannabis product pricing across product classes, excise duty, COGS, regulatory fee allocation, provincial wholesale models, estimated MSRP, and competitive positioning.

## What it does

CannaCost Canada helps answer the core commercial question:

> Can this SKU actually make money once excise, board markup, regulatory costs, freight, and retail pricing are factored in?

The calculator is designed to support early-stage SKU planning, product-call preparation, wholesale pricing conversations, margin modelling, and competitive pricing analysis.

## Key features

- Health Canada product class selection aligned to Schedule 4 Cannabis Act categories
- Format-specific pricing logic for dried flower, pre-rolls, infused pre-rolls, vapes, concentrates, edibles, beverages, topicals, plants, and seeds
- CRA-style excise duty calculations
- THC-mg based excise logic for extracts, edibles, and topicals
- Gram-based excise logic for dried and fresh cannabis
- COGS modelling by product format
- Other per-unit costs including freight, go-to-market spend, and partner fees
- Health Canada annual regulatory fee allocation per SKU
- Province-specific wholesale and MSRP modelling
- OCS, BCLDB, AGLC, SQDC, and other provincial board model references
- Estimated national MSRP table
- Competitive set input and comparison
- Auto-parse field for copied retailer product listings
- Export summary prompt for pitch deck or internal pricing review

## How to use

1. Select the correct Health Canada product class.
2. Choose the product format or subcategory.
3. Enter the product name and home province.
4. Add excise inputs such as grams, THC mg, dutiable amount, or unit count.
5. Enter product-specific COGS.
6. Add freight, go-to-market, partner fees, and target margin.
7. Enter producer revenue and annual SKU unit volume for regulatory fee allocation.
8. Review the pricing chain, gross margin, provincial MSRP table, and competitive set.
9. Export the summary for internal pricing review, pitch decks, or product-call preparation.

## Compliance and accuracy disclaimer

CannaCost Canada is a commercial planning tool, not legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, or financial advice.

The calculator is intended to support pricing strategy and internal modelling. Users should verify all calculations, assumptions, provincial markups, excise rules, tax treatment, regulatory fees, and product classification decisions with qualified legal, accounting, regulatory, or tax professionals before relying on the results for submissions, contracts, financial reporting, or launch decisions.

Cannabis regulations, provincial board policies, excise duty rules, taxes, markups, and product-call requirements may change. Users are responsible for confirming the most current requirements.
